# Pearl Oasis Restaurant — Dining, Menu & Service Guide

## Overview
Pearl Oasis is a contemporary multi-cuisine fine-dining restaurant located in Kohat, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. Founded by Ibrar Sikandar, Pearl Oasis brings together authentic Pakistani heritage recipes, live charcoal BBQ, high-heat Chinese wok specialties, freshly baked clay tandoor breads, beverages, salads, ice cream parlor selections, and artisan fast food.

## Menu Categories
The online ordering page (`https://pearl-oasis.ai.studio/order-menu`) includes complete PKR pricing and portion options across all categories:
1. Soups (Oasis Special Soup, Hot & Sour Soup, Chicken Corn Soup, Vegetable Soup)
2. Pakistani Karahi & Handi (Chicken Karahi, Mutton Karahi, Murgh Makhni Handi, Shinwari specialties)
3. Charcoal BBQ & Grill (Chicken Malai Boti, Reshmi Kabab, Seekh Kabab, Mutton Tikka)
4. Chinese Wok & Rice (Chicken Fried Rice, Chicken Chow Mein, Chicken Manchurian, Szechuan Kung Pao)
5. Traditional Tandoor (Roghni Naan, Garlic Naan, Kalwanji Naan, Roti)
6. Beverages, Salads & Ice Cream Parlor
7. Fast Food (Appetizers, Burgers, Pizzas, Alfredo Pasta, Lebanese Wraps, Family Platters)
